# Data attribution

- Candidate lists, programmes, statistics and candidate declarations: Centrālā
  vēlēšanu komisija (Central Election Commission of Latvia), https://dati.cvk.lv/SV2026/.
  Published under the Saeima Election Law; the candidate is responsible for the
  accuracy of their own declaration.
- Parliamentary records (when present): Latvijas Republikas Saeima,
  https://titania.saeima.lv, and Delna / Transparency International Latvia,
  https://deputatiuzdelnas.lv.
- Wikidata (when present): CC0, https://www.wikidata.org.
- Party registry (when present): Korupcijas novēršanas un apkarošanas birojs,
  https://info.knab.gov.lv/parties.

Derived fields (experience tier, tags, aggregates) are computed by this project
and carry no authority beyond the rules in `pipeline/rules/`.
